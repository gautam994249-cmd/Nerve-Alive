Alpha-Lipoic Acid (ALA):

Alpha-Lipoic Acid is a potent antioxidant that neutralizes free radicals in both water-soluble and fat-soluble environments. By combating cellular oxidative stress, ALA helps reduce systemic inflammation surrounding sensitive nerve endings, effectively lessening burning, numbness, and tingling

Methylcobalamin (Vitamin B12)

Methylcobalamin is the bioactive, coenzyme form of Vitamin B12 directly utilized by the nervous system. It plays an essential role in repairing damaged myelin sheaths, improving neural conduction velocity, and preventing long-term nerve fiber degeneration.

Benfotiamine (Vitamin B1 derivative)

Benfotiamine is a fat-soluble derivative of thiamine that boasts significantly higher bioavailability than standard Vitamin B1. It inhibits harmful advanced glycation end-products (AGEs) that damage micro-blood vessels and nerve structures.

Acetyl-L-Carnitine is an amino acid derivative that assists fatty acid transport into cellular mitochondria to fuel nerve energy metabolism. It promotes nerve cell regeneration, relieves neuropathic discomfort, and enhances peripheral sensory response
